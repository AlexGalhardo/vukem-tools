---
title: "CLI"
description: "vukem-tools tools, pdf crop, mcp, and serve."
---

```text
vukem-tools tools
vukem-tools pdf crop --input in.pdf --output out.pdf
vukem-tools mcp
vukem-tools serve
```

`tools` writes the catalogue as JSON. `pdf crop` writes `output`, `cropped`, and `bytes`. `mcp` speaks JSON-RPC on stdin and stdout until the pipe closes. `serve` listens on `127.0.0.1:8787` unless you pass another address, and serves `GET /tools` and `POST /pdf/crop`.

Flags for the crop are `--input`, `--output`, `--left`, `--bottom`, `--right`, and `--top`. `--input` and `--output` are required.
